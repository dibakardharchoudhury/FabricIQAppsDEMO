# Maintenance feasibility

- Read existing work, inspections, maintenance notifications and recorded spare-part stock.
- Stock at or below the recorded reorder level merits a factual stock-risk observation, not a diagnosis.
- Quantity on hand does not establish unreserved usable availability.
- Equipment type/category does not prove exact part compatibility. Require authoritative BOM or compatibility evidence.
- Do not invent reservations, substitutes, supplier lead times, task durations or crew availability.
- An outage recommendation requires qualified procedures, operating/dispatch constraints, redundancy, calendar and duration evidence.
- Telemetry BAD quality and open work alone do not prove downtime.
- If required planning evidence is absent, state the specific blockers rather than giving a fabricated feasible schedule.
- Identify uncovered work only from verified equipment evidence, complete open-work coverage, and the assigned
  investigation domain. Do not use an unrelated inspection, notification, parts record, telemetry condition, or
  maintenance issue merely because it concerns the same equipment.
- Do not order/reserve parts, stop equipment, schedule an outage, send notifications or create SQL records.
- The Work Order specialist may stage an editable review card; only explicit human approval authorizes submission.
